Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information MecQuad collects through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We follow the principles of Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022.

Who we are

MecQuad is a technology company based in Sri Lanka that builds software, AI, data and digital marketing solutions. You can contact us about privacy at info@mecquad.tech or +94 77 866 2627.

What we collect

  • Information you give us when you fill in the demo request form: your name, company, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, the solution you are interested in, and your message.
  • Messages you send us by email, WhatsApp or phone.
  • Basic technical data such as your IP address and browser type, which our hosting provider records automatically to deliver and protect the website.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this website. We do not ask for payment details here.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a demo or consultation.
  • To prepare a proposal or quotation you asked for.
  • To deliver and support a project if you become our client.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.

We only use your information for these purposes. We never sell it, and we do not add you to marketing lists without your permission.

Services that help us

We use a few trusted services to run this website and reply to you. They process your information only to provide their service:

  • Vercel hosts this website.
  • FormSubmit delivers the demo request form to our email inbox.
  • Google provides our email (Gmail) and the fonts used on this website.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) if you choose to message us on WhatsApp.

Some of these services store data on servers outside Sri Lanka.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to 2 years so we can follow up on your request. If you become a client, we keep project records for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal requirements. You can ask us to delete your details sooner.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is wrong or out of date.
  • Delete your information.
  • Stop using your information, or withdraw your consent at any time.

Email info@mecquad.tech with your request. We will reply within 21 working days.

Keeping it safe

We protect your information with secure, password-protected accounts and share it only with people at MecQuad who need it to help you.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

Questions?

Email info@mecquad.tech or WhatsApp +94 77 866 2627.
