The story behind our name

MecQuad comes from E = MC².

A small amount of mass holds a huge amount of energy. Every business idea works the same way. Our job is to release that energy.

Mass: your idea

The raw potential. A process to automate, a product to launch, data waiting to be understood.

C

C: our capability

Engineering, AI and design skill that turns the idea into something that works.

²

Squared: the impact

Measurable results. Faster teams, smarter decisions, more customers.
